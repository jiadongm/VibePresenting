## Final checks

- [ ] Slide count fits the time budget.
- [ ] Opening establishes the question and audience contract.
- [ ] Each technical term appears before it is relied upon.
- [ ] Source notes and attributions are legible and present.
- [ ] Assumptions have concrete scientific failure modes.
- [ ] The final slide returns to the central thesis.
- [ ] Manual edits and accepted slide wording have been preserved.
